The room

Who has voted, and what they picked. One line per person, their current choice only — a member who changed their mind appears once, with the pick that counts.

This is the only place the standings exist before Sunday night. The site itself shows no numbers at all while a ballot is open — not to members, not to you — so that nobody votes for the side that is winning. Keep the key to yourself and this stays true.

The key is FF_KEY in the Netlify environment variables, and it is remembered in this browser once you have typed it. Chair beside a name means the counter recognises that person as you, from FF_ME — that is the vote that gets withdrawn if the room ties exactly.
