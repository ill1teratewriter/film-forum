FILM FORUM — one file, one line
================================

  chapters.js     <- top level, replaces the one in your repo

WHAT CHANGED
------------

Chapter 4's discussion date, and nothing else:

    date: '2026-10-25'   ->   date: '2026-10-26'

That moves this week's forum to Monday 26 October, 6:00 PM Pacific
(9:00 PM Eastern). Everything after it is untouched -- chapter 5 is
still Sunday 8 November, and the normal Sunday rhythm carries on by
itself. There is nothing to undo next week.


CAREFUL IF YOU EDIT BY HAND
---------------------------

"2026-10-25" appears TWICE in chapters.js. A find-and-replace would
break the site.

  line 78   date: '2026-10-25',              <- change this one
  line 101  opens: '2026-10-25T18:30:00-07:00'   <- LEAVE THIS

Line 101 is when chapter 5's ballot opened. It is already open and
closes midnight into Sunday 1 November. Moving it would hide the two
Halloween films from people who have already seen them.


THE CALENDAR LOOKS AFTER ITSELF
-------------------------------

netlify.toml runs `node tools/build-calendar.mjs` on every deploy, so
the .ics invitation and netlify/functions/ballots.mjs are both rebuilt
from the new date automatically. You do not have to run anything.
